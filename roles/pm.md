Cares about: status, effort, blockers, dependencies, timeline, risk.
Jargon OK: planning terms (sprint, estimate, blocker, milestone). No code terms.
Leave out: code and implementation detail.
Technical part: the work left and who does it, dependencies, the numbers behind the estimate. No code.
End with: effort/timeline impact and who needs to act.
